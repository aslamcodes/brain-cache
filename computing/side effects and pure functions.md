Side effect is a adjective to a expression or a operation. A operation or expression is said to have a side effect if it mutates states or depends on a shared state.

**Examples from wikipedia**
Example side effects include modifying a non-local variable, a static local variable or a mutable argument passed by reference; performing I/O; or calling other functions with side-effects
